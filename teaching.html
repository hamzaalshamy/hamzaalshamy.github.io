Teaching and mentorship

I've been fortunate to receive guidance from mentors who shaped the trajectory of my career. They made a career path in research visible where it previously wasn't, and showed me its norms and how to navigate it. That experience made me aware of the impact of setting a culture of giving as you have received. If someone can learn or benefit from my experiences, I'm always happy to chat! I'm especially sympathetic to students who want to do interdisciplinary research, as it's sometimes difficult to find a home for that. Also, being from Saudi Arabia myself, I’m especially happy to hear from Saudi students, whether to chat, get some insight, or bounce ideas around.

Additionally, I'm very passionate about teaching and pedagogy!
