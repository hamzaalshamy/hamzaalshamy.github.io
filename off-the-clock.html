Music

Music is a big part of my daily life. I've been playing guitar for over ten years, mostly neo-soul, jazz, and blues. I build layered progressions with a looper and a few pedals. This was my introduction to looping.

Current rig

My guitar runs through a few effect pedals and a loop pedal. These are the ones I'm using right now, not always in this order.

Press play, then click a pedal to switch it, drag the wah, or tap the looper to record two bars and play over them.
